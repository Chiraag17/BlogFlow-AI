# 3D Golden Stardust Cosmos & Post-Login Warp Transition Plan

## Overview
We will elevate the Login & Signup experience (`/login` and `/signup`) with an interactive **3D Golden Stardust Cosmos** canvas, **keystroke energy particles** that flow toward the 3D Gutenberg cube as you type, and a **3D Cube Starfield Warp Jump** animation that triggers upon successful authentication before directing to `/studio`.

---

## 1. Interactive 3D Golden Stardust Cosmos Background
- **Real-Time 3D Starfield Canvas**:
  - Render a smooth 60fps HTML5 `<canvas>` starfield populated with multi-depth golden brass (`#E5C98B`, `#C8A15A`), warm ivory (`#F7F4EC`), and emerald-tinted stars drifting in 3D perspective space ($Z$-depth projection).
  - **Cursor Parallax & Gravity**: Moving the mouse gently tilts the 3D starfield camera and illuminates nearby constellation dust.
  - **Cosmic Nebula Clouds**: Layer soft, breathing golden-emerald nebula gradients behind the 3D starfield that adapt cleanly to both Dark (deep obsidian cosmos) and Light (celestial ivory parchment cosmos) themes.

## 2. Keystroke Energy Particles Flowing into the 3D Cube
- **Interactive Typing Bursts**:
  - Every keystroke in the Email, Name, Password, or OTP inputs spawns luminous golden stardust particles that arc across the screen and absorb into the 3D Gutenberg cube, causing the cube's orbital rings to pulse with golden energy.

## 3. 3D Post-Login Success Transition (Cube Spin & Starfield Warp Jump)
- **Choreographed 3D Launch Sequence (~1.8s)**:
  1. **Form Dissolve & Center Stage**: As soon as Sign In, Sign Up, Demo Login, or Password Reset login succeeds, the form card smoothly dissolves and the 3D Gutenberg cube glides to the center of the viewport.
  2. **Rapid 3D Spin & Seal Lock**: The 3D cube accelerates into a multi-axis 3D spin surrounded by expanding golden shockwave rings, locking onto the glowing golden **Approval Seal** face.
  3. **Starfield Warp Jump**: The 3D starfield accelerates into high-speed radial warp streaks (`warpSpeed` multiplier ramping up $25\times$) with a golden light tunnel zoom that seamlessly transitions the user into `/studio`.
